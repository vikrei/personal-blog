Learning to Slow Down

Life Thoughts

Sometimes productivity is not about doing more, but about giving yourself enough space to think.

I used to think that a good day had to be filled with tasks, plans and visible progress. If I slowed down, it felt like I was wasting time.

Lately I have started to notice that some of my best ideas appear when I am not actively trying to be productive. A quiet walk, making coffee, or just sitting without doing anything can sometimes create more clarity than another hour in front of a screen.
